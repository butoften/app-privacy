适用范围

本政策适用于「调音台」（Audio Mixer，Bundle ID：audio-mixer.butof.com）。本 App 用于导入、整理和多轨播放音频，无需注册账号。

音频、视频与本地处理

你通过系统文件选择器、相册视频选择器、其他 App 分享或局域网网页主动提供素材。视频在设备本地提取音频；不兼容的格式在导入阶段转换。素材库只保存最终音频，不保留输入视频或额外的原始音频副本。接收与处理过程中可以存在临时输入和未完成输出；导入流程结束或下次启动时清理。App 不删除相册、系统文件位置或电脑中的源文件。

本地保存与删除

素材、文件夹索引、声轨名称、绑定关系、音量、循环和布局设置保存在设备的应用目录中。可以在素材库删除素材，或通过系统删除 App 清理应用数据；系统备份可能按你的设备设置包含应用数据。未完成的局域网上传为支持断点续传会暂存，可以在 App 中手动清理；超过七天的任务在服务清理时过期移除。

权限与音频输出

相册和文件选择仅用于导入你选择的素材。本地网络权限用于电脑上传及管理。后台音频能力用于继续播放你已启动的声音；蓝牙音箱通过系统音频路由输出。App 不提供录音，不采集麦克风音频或电话对话。你可以在系统设置中管理相关权限。

局域网配对与浏览器数据

仅在你主动开启时启动设备上的 HTTP 服务器。电脑输入 App 显示的配对码后，可以查看素材名称、文件夹和上传状态，并上传、重命名、移动或删除素材。内容在电脑与运行 App 的设备之间传输，不经过我们的云服务器。关闭服务器后配对会话失效。浏览器保存用于验证配对的会话 Cookie；上传页面使用浏览器本地存储记录续传信息。HTTP 不加密传输，请在可信局域网使用，结束后关闭服务。

开发者的数据处理

App 不内置广告、跟踪或第三方用户行为分析 SDK，不将你的素材或使用记录上传给开发者。若你主动发送支持邮件，我们会收到邮箱地址、邮件内容及附件，仅用于处理支持请求。请勿发送不必要的私密素材；可通过支持邮箱申请删除支持邮件中的个人信息，但依法需要保留的记录除外。

TestFlight 测试版本

使用 TestFlight 时，Apple 会按其政策处理测试安装、使用、崩溃和反馈数据，并可能向开发者提供测试信息。你主动提交的反馈或截图也可能提供给开发者。这与 App 本身的本地素材处理不同。详情见 Apple 的 TestFlight 隐私说明。

政策更新与联系

本政策更新时会调整生效日期。如对数据处理有疑问，请联系 service@butof.com。

English

Scope

This policy applies to Audio Mixer (调音台, bundle ID: audio-mixer.butof.com), an app for importing, organizing and playing multiple audio tracks. No account is required.

Media and on-device processing

You choose media through the system file picker, photo video picker, sharing from another app, or the local-network upload page. Video audio extraction and format conversion take place on your device. The library retains only the final audio, not the input video or an extra original audio copy. Temporary inputs and incomplete outputs may exist while processing and are cleaned up when the import ends or on the next launch. Source files in Photos, external file locations and your computer are not deleted.

Local storage and deletion

Media, folder indexes, track names, bindings, volume, loop and layout settings are stored in the app directory. Delete media in the library or delete the app through system settings to remove its local data. System backups may include app data depending on your device settings. Incomplete network uploads are retained for resuming; you can clear them manually. Tasks older than seven days expire during service cleanup.

Permissions and audio output

Photo and file selection are used to import media you select. Local-network access supports computer uploads and management. Background audio continues playback you start; Bluetooth speakers use the system audio route. The app does not record microphone audio or telephone conversations. Manage relevant permissions in system settings.

Local-network pairing and browser storage

An HTTP server runs on your device only when you enable it. After entering the pairing code displayed in the app, a computer can see media names, folders and upload status, and upload, rename, move or delete media. Transfers take place between the computer and your device, not through our cloud servers. Stopping the server invalidates pairing sessions. The browser stores a session cookie for pairing and local upload-resume information. HTTP traffic is not encrypted: use a trusted local network and stop the server when finished.

Developer access to data

The app has no advertising, tracking or third-party usage analytics SDKs and does not send your media or usage records to the developer. If you email support, we receive your address, message and attachments to handle your request. Avoid sending unnecessary private media. You can request deletion of personal information in support emails, except records that must be retained by law.

TestFlight versions

When you use TestFlight, Apple processes beta installation, usage, crash and feedback information under its own policy and may share beta information with the developer. Feedback and screenshots you submit may also be shared with the developer. This is separate from the app’s local media processing. See Apple’s TestFlight privacy notice for details.

Updates and contact

We update the effective date when this policy changes. For privacy questions, email service@butof.com.
